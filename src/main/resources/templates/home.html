About TSEC Mumbai

Thakur Shyamnarayan Engineering College (TSEC), managed by Zagdu Singh Charitable Trust (ZCT), is a premier technical institution committed to nurturing future-ready engineers and innovators.

Cutting-Edge Programs

Offering Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs in Computer Engineering, CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning), Information Technology, Electronics & Computer Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering.

World-Class Infrastructure

Equipped with advanced AR/VR laboratories, high-performance computing centers, modern workshop facilities, smart digital classrooms, and comprehensive central library resources.

Training, Placement & Innovation

Active industry collaborations, dedicated Training & Placement Cell with eminent recruiters, Institution's Innovation Council (IIC-TSEC), and student chapters of ISTE and DLLE.

Approvals & Accreditations

Recognized by Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Government of Maharashtra, approved by AICTE New Delhi, and permanently affiliated to the University of Mumbai.
